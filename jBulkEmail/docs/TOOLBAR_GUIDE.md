## How It Works

- **Visual Editing**: You won't see `<b>text</b>` anymore. You will see **text**.
- **Images**: Images show up directly in the box.
- **HTML Checkbox**: Automatically turns ON when you use any button. Keep it checked to send bold/images.

## Shortcuts

- `Shift+Tab`: Move text left (Back Tab)
- `Tab`: Move text right
- `Ctrl+B / Ctrl+I`: Bold / Italic
- `Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y`: Undo / Redo

---

The email will look exactly as you see it in the editor! 🚀
